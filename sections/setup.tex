\textbf{Model.} We use direction 1's model with per-node acceptance. Each node $v$ belongs to a group $g(v)\in\{H,L\}$ (high and low receptiveness) with mean acceptance $\mu_g$, and $a_v=\mathrm{clip}(\mu_{g(v)}+\sigma z_v,0,1)$, $z_v\sim N(0,1)$, so groups differ on average, individuals vary, and the population is bimodal once $|\mu_H-\mu_L|>2\sigma$. In each realization $v$ accepts the truth with probability $a_v$; if both campaigns reach $v$ in the same step, the truth wins only if $v$ accepts. Truth seeds always spread the truth, and each direction of an edge is live independently. $S_N$ and $S_P$ are the rumor and truth seeds, $\mathcal{C}$ the communities (in the two-group experiments, $H$ and $L$ themselves), and $\mathcal{S}=\{S\subseteq V\setminus S_N:|S|\le k\}$ the feasible seed sets.

\textbf{Saved and converted.} A node is \emph{exposed} if the rumor reaches it with $S_P=\emptyset$, \emph{saved} if it is exposed but the rumor no longer reaches it, and \emph{converted} if it is saved and believes the truth. A rejecting node can be saved without being converted when truth nodes cut every rumor path to it (direction 2's $w$). Following FWRRS \cite{fwrrs}, with expectations over edge and acceptance coins,
\begin{align}
s^-_c(S_N,S_P) &= \mathbb{E}\,|\{v \in c \setminus S_N : v \text{ ends rumor-active}\}|, \\
E_c &= s^-_c(S_N,\emptyset),\quad B_c = E_c - s^-_c(S_N,S_P), \\
r_c &= B_c/E_c,\quad B=\textstyle\sum_c B_c,\quad E=\textstyle\sum_c E_c.
\end{align}
Counting converted instead of saved nodes gives $r_c^{\mathrm{conv}}$ (vs.\ $r_c^{\mathrm{sav}}$). Rates are over $\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{infected}}=\{c:E_c>0\}$, since otherwise $r_c=0/0$. The rumor ignores acceptance, so exposure is independent of $v$'s acceptance coin and
\begin{gather*}
\mathbb{E}[\text{converted}_c]\le \bar a_c E_c+\mathbb{E}\,|S_P\cap \text{exposed}_c|,\\
\bar a_c=\frac{\sum_{v\in c}a_v\Pr[v\text{ exposed}]}{E_c},
\end{gather*}
i.e.\ the converted rate is at most the exposure-weighted mean acceptance plus the seeds' own share. No such cap holds for the saved rate.

\textbf{Ceiling model.} Two groups $H,L$ with mean acceptances $a_L\le a_H$ (the $\bar a_g$ above), $N=E_H+E_L$, $w_g=E_g/N$, $r=a_H/a_L$. For $g\in\{H,L\}$, $k_g$ is the (continuous) number of seeds placed in $g$, with $k_H+k_L=k$. Each seed in $g$ reaches $b$ nodes of $g$ only and converts $b\,a_g$ of them, so $r_g=\min(b\,a_g k_g/E_g,\;a_g)$. $L$ saturates ($r_L=a_L$) at $k_L=E_L/b$, which equal rates reach at $k^*=\frac{E_L}{b}\bigl(1+\frac{E_H a_L}{E_L a_H}\bigr)$.

\emph{Claim 1 (below saturation, DP $=$ maximin).} Maximin equalizes rates, $k_g=\rho E_g/(b a_g)$ with $\rho=bk/\sum_g(E_g/a_g)$, valid while $\rho\le a_L$ ($k\le k^*$). DP's best equal-rate allocation is the same, since its total $\rho N$ rises with $\rho$. If $bk\le E_H$, the unconstrained optimum seeds only $H$, so both pay $\mathrm{PoF}=w_H+r\,w_L$, and $L$, a share $w_L$ of the exposure, receives a share $r\,w_L/(w_H+r\,w_L)$ of the budget. The ceiling model ignores shielding and seed overlap; the simulations test how far these move the predictions.

\textbf{Experimental setup.} Both sections use one network drawn from a two-group stochastic block model (SBM): $350$ nodes in $H$ and $150$ in $L$, with each pair of nodes joined independently with probability $p_{in}=0.05$ within a group and $p_{out}=0.01$ across groups, so each group is a homophilous community. Messages cross each edge with IC probability $0.08$. There are $5$ random rumor seeds, $\mu_H=0.8$ and $\sigma=0.1$, with the same network, rumor seeds, biases and realizations throughout. Seeds are chosen on $100$ sampled realizations and evaluated on $200$ others. Every method spends exactly $k$ seeds, and the greedy methods choose them from the $50$ highest-degree nodes in each group, excluding rumor seeds. Each method is run once per outcome (saved, converted) and scored on it. \emph{Total} is the fraction of exposed nodes counted by the outcome; PoF, the price of fairness, is fairness-blind \emph{total greedy}'s total divided by the method's total ($1$ means no cost; $1.35$ means total greedy blocks $35\%$ more). Truth seeds count toward their own group's rate. All results come from one network, one set of rumor seeds and one draw of biases, so differences of about $0.01$ are noise.
